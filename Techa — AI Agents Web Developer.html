    07 — FAQ
    
Good questions.

    

      
What is an AI agent website?
A website with an AI assistant that can understand visitors, answer questions from your content and take actions like collecting leads or booking meetings.

      
Which tools do you use?
Three.js and GSAP for the visuals, modern JavaScript for the site, and large language model APIs for the agents, connected to your tools through integrations.

      
How long does a project take?
A focused site with one agent usually takes two to four weeks. Larger builds with several agents take longer.

      
Is it mobile friendly?
Yes. Every layout and 3D scene is adapted for phones, tablets and laptops.
